I'm a PhD student in the UKRI CDT Biomedical AI, University of Edinburgh, supervised by Prof. Miguel O. Bernabeu (Centre for Medical Informatics, Usher Institute) and Prof. Amos Storkey ([Bayeswatch Group](https://bayeswatch.com/), School of Informatics).

I primarily work on applying machine learning to retinal imaging (pictures of the back of the eye) to automatically detect eye disease and to predict systemic disease risk (e.g. cardio- and neuro-vascular disease). I'm also interested in the broader application of machine learning to biomedical data (e.g. electronic health records, cell microscopy, ECGs, etc.).

Before coming to Edinburgh, I completed an MSc in Business Administration (Humboldt University Berlin) and a BA in Philosophy, Politics and Economics (University of Oxford), and interned in management consulting (BCG) and at some startups.
