Exploring Resolution in Retinal Imaging

Discover how image resolution and scaling methods affect the quality and clarity of retinal images.

Fundus Image 1 | 1024px | Bilinear Downscale | Bilinear Upscale

Yes, resolution plays a significant role in image clarity and detail. Higher resolutions capture more information, making it easier to analyze fine structures. However, they also require more storage and computational resources.

Even lower resolutions can be surprisingly informative, as they can highlight key patterns while reducing storage and processing demands. The choice of scaling methods also impacts how well details are preserved during resizing.

For instance, a 1024x1024 JPEG image compressed with 85% quality might appear worse than an uncompressed 768x768 image due to artifacts from compression and scaling.

The example images used here come from the iChallenge GAMMA dataset and demo images from Choroidalyzer. Explore and observe how resolution and scaling methods affect image quality!
