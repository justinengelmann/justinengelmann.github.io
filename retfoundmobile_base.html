About RETFoundMobile

RETFoundMobile brings the power of retinal foundation models directly to your browser. This tool runs RETFoundGreen locally on your device's hardware, whether you're using a phone, tablet, or computer.

Since everything runs in your browser, no images are ever sent to a server. Your privacy is guaranteed because your data never leaves your device. You can verify this yourself by loading the page and then turning off your internet connection - the tool will continue to work!
